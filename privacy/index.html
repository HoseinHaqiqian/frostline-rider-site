Frostline Rider privacy policy

Effective: 2026-11-10

The short version

We, the makers of Frostline Rider, do not collect, receive or sell any information about you or your child.

  • No accounts, no logins, no names, no email addresses.
  • No ads of any kind.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting. The only outside code in the game is the store's own purchase library (Google Play Billing or Apple's StoreKit), for the one optional purchase. It talks to Google or Apple, not to us, and Google's library also sends Google technical records of its own use (see "Internet, ads and tracking").
  • The game's own code does not use the internet. Everything works offline; only buying (or restoring) the optional purchase needs the store.
  • Game progress is saved only on this device.

Who we are

Frostline Rider is made by Hosein Haqiqian. Questions about this policy: studionenay@gmail.com.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps one save file on this device, in the app's own private storage. It holds:

  • Game progress: runs finished, stars, coins earned in the game, stickers, animals found, outfits, and where a run was paused so it can carry on.
  • The kids on this device: each kid is only a picture avatar (for example a fox or a penguin) and their progress. We never ask for a name, age, photo or any other detail.
  • Settings: sound, music, calm camera, battery saver, the steering style, and the picture quality the game chose for this device.
  • Whether the optional purchase was made on this device (just yes or no), so the extra mountains stay open offline. No order number, receipt or account detail is kept.

This file never leaves the device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. A grown-up can also remove a kid's progress in the game's settings (behind the grown-ups check).

Your device may include app data in its own backups (for example an iCloud or Google device backup), if you have turned that on. Those backups are run by Apple or Google under your own account; we never receive them.

What the game uses on the device, and does not keep

  • Motion sensor: if a grown-up picks the "tilt" steering style, the game reads how the device is tilted to steer the rider. It is used live, moment to moment, and is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: short buzzes when something happens in the game (on Android this needs the "vibrate" permission). Nothing is collected.
  • Game controllers: If a controller is connected, menus may respond to it. Nothing is collected.

The game does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts, photos or the device's advertising ID.

Internet, ads and tracking

The game's own code makes no network connections and never sends anything to us. It contains no ads, no analytics, no social features, no chat, and no links that open web pages.

The one exception is the store's own purchase system, which is part of Google Play (Google Play Billing) or the App Store (Apple's StoreKit). The game asks it, when it starts and when you come back to it, whether the purchase was made on your store account, and asks it for the price when a grown-up opens the purchase screen. To answer, Google Play or the App Store may contact Google or Apple. That is handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us.

On Android, Google's billing library (part of the app because of the purchase) adds the internet and network-state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) permissions to the app; the game's own code does not use them. The library also sends Google technical logs about how it is used, for example whether a request to the store worked or failed. These go to Google under Google's privacy policy; we do not receive them, and the game puts nothing about your child in them.

Purchases

The game has one optional purchase. It opens every mountain and the Journey for every kid on this device; it never sells coins, outfits or anything else, and there are no subscriptions. On iPhone and iPad it works with Family Sharing.

  • Children never see a price and are never asked to buy or to ask a parent. A locked mountain is just shown dimmed; tapping it does nothing. The purchase is only in the settings, which sit behind a grown-ups check, and then behind a second, harder check (a multiplication written in words, answered on a keypad; three misses lock it for a minute, even if the game is closed). Only after that does a screen for the grown-up show the store's price, what the purchase opens, Buy and Restore.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store under the parent's account. We never see card details, names, email addresses or account details. The store only tells the game whether the purchase was made, and the game keeps only that yes or no, on this device.
  • Restore: if the game is installed again, or on another device with the same store account, "Restore purchase" asks the store again. Nothing needs to be typed in the game.
  • Parents can also require approval for purchases in the store itself (Ask to Buy on Apple devices, purchase approvals in Google Play).

Children

The game is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete on our side. If you think we hold information about your child, contact us at studionenay@gmail.com and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game (grown-ups area) and on the store page, with a new date. We will never start collecting information from children without first asking a parent and updating this policy.
